Mapping As-Is vs. To-Be Workflows That Teams Actually Use

Category: Consulting & Analysis • Published: August 2026

Process diagrams are the universal language of systems design. However, overly complex flowchart diagrams with dozens of decision diamonds often confuse non-technical stakeholders.

Best Practices for Clear Workflow Diagrams

  • Use Swimlanes: Group steps clearly by user role or system boundary (e.g., User, Portal, System Automation, Admin).
  • Focus on Exceptional Paths: Document the "happy path" first, but explicitly map out error handling branches (e.g., rejected approvals or system timeouts).
  • Keep It High-Level for Business: Create a summary executive diagram for business leads and a detailed BPMN flow for the engineering team.
