Translating Ambiguous Business Needs into Technical Requirements

Category: Consulting & Analysis • Published: August 2026

Stakeholders often express their needs in terms of solutions ("We need a new dashboard") rather than underlying pain points ("We lack visibility into weekly ticket resolution bottlenecks"). The core role of a Business Systems Analyst is bridging this communication gap.

3 Diagnostic Questions to Ask in Discovery Workshops

  • "What manual workaround are you currently using when this step fails?"
  • "What specific metric or outcome defines success for this feature?"
  • "Who needs access to this information, and what action will they take once they see it?"
